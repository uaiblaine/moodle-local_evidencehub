Evidence hub as a data report

Mockup for data-report-study.md. The hub's list on the fleet's data report mould: Report Builder underneath, a band of figures, the filter bar with its panel and menus, a bounded grid or cards, a pager, and core's own table as the fallback. The data is invented. The tile "Expiring in 30 days", the second panel and the "Valid until" facet are new figures (decisions 7 and 12). Press the bar's buttons to open their layers.

What is the same as today

  • A row is a worker and a competency of one learning plan template.
  • "Needs attention" is the default; a restricted pair shows its status and nothing else.
  • The inspection drawer opens from the row; the worker's name leads to their evidence page.
  • The workers hidden by permissions are counted.

What changes

  • Filters are core's stored filters, set in the panel, shown as chips; the search and the scope are not stored.
  • The reader chooses columns and their widths; the download follows them.
  • The status tiles become a distribution; pressing a line sets the status filter.
  • The download is what the list shows, never ticked rows (decision 9); no More menu on the bar.
  • The matrix is reached from View (decision 11).

Open in this mockup

  • The status labels' tones (decision 15): the Design System has no dark tone, which "Expired" uses today.
  • The counts in the scope's cells come from the status facet.
